# Golden Paths

These are the workflows GPT-Codex protects first:

- Buyer browses cards, opens a detail page, adds a card to cart, and reaches checkout.
- Honoree scans QR, enters PIN, and sees the provenance reveal.
- Buyer adds a person and an occasion, then finds a card for that occasion.
- Artist submits a card for approval.
- Admin approves users, artists, and cards.
- Admin reviews orders, fraud flags, and platform health.
